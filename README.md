# SpaceGuard
<img width="700" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/18c5d2e3-ef16-416c-bdee-5d687b06be99" />

With the advent of humanity's space travel becoming available to more people, there has become a need for people to rescue those who get stuck in space.

Your mission is to grab people from space and deliver them to the space station they want to go to.

### Controls
**SHIP:**

WASD [Movement]

Space/Shift [Up/Down]

F (hold) [Get Out]

**Out of Ship:**

WASD [Movement]

Q/E [Rotate]

Space/Shift [Up/Down]

F (hold) [Get In]

CTRL [Stop]
